`text, captions, watermark, blurry, distorted, deformed hands, extra fingers, low quality, dark, gloomy`

### 01 — Intro / establishing
Bright, clean modern electronics factory in India, camera slowly glides forward down rows of tidy assembly workstations where many workers in white ESD coats, gloves and masks are busy, spacious well-lit facility, corporate cinematography, shallow depth of field, 4k.

### 02 — Assembly (robot arm)
Extreme macro close-up, a robotic arm precisely places a tiny component onto a green circuit board while a gloved female operator in an ESD coat guides the line, soft bokeh background of a bright electronics factory, static locked-off camera, 4k.

### 03 — Defects (phone PCB)
Close-up, a female quality inspector in ESD coat and mask examines a smartphone motherboard PCB under a magnifier lamp, notices a fault and drops it into a bright red reject tray, slow push-in camera, bright modern factory, 4k.

### 04 — Overproduction
Slow sweeping pan across tall warehouse shelves overflowing with stacked finished-product boxes in a bright electronics factory storage area, clearly far too much stock, corporate cinematography, 4k.

### 05 — Waiting
A woman worker in a white ESD coat stands idle beside a paused machine, arms crossed, waiting and glancing at it, other workers blurred behind, medium static shot with a slight slow drift, bright factory, 4k.

### 06 — Non-utilized talent
Close-up on the face of a thoughtful female engineer in an ESD coat as she pauses with a bright idea, looking up, while a manager walks past in the background without noticing, shallow focus, slow camera, bright factory, 4k.

### 07 — Transportation
Smooth tracking shot following a worker pushing a trolley loaded with parts across a long bright factory floor, travelling a large distance between stations, modern clean electronics facility, 4k.

### 08 — Inventory
Slow push-in on rows of plastic bins and trays packed high with work-in-progress parts piled up beside an assembly line in a bright electronics factory, static framing, 4k.

### 09 — Motion
Tight close-up on the hands and arms of an operator at a cluttered workbench, reaching, stretching and bending repeatedly to search for a tool, wasted movement, bright factory, steady camera, 4k.

### 10 — Extra processing
Macro close-up of gloved hands over-polishing and re-wiping an already-clean metal part far longer than needed, unnecessary effort, bright electronics factory, static camera, 4k.

### 11 — 5S tool-board
Slow pan close-up across a pristine organized 5S shadow-board of hand tools, each tool in its outlined labelled place, spotless, a worker's hand returning one tool to its slot, bright factory, 4k.

### 12 — Outro
Sweeping rising crane shot over a bright, organized, efficient electronics factory floor with neat workstations and busy workers in ESD coats, inspiring optimistic mood, warm ambient light, 4k.
